Rotisserie Chicken, Mushroom & Rice Casserole

🍳 Food American Moderate
15 minPrep
1 hr 25 minCook
8Servings
📖 Adapted from EatingWell

First recipe up: a weeknight casserole built around a rotisserie chicken from the grocery store, which does most of the work for you (leftover rotisserie chicken works just as well).

Ingredients

  • 4 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
  • 1 tsp finely chopped fresh thyme, plus leaves for garnish
  • 10 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 3.5 oz maitake mushrooms, torn into clusters
  • 2 medium shallots, finely chopped (about 3/4 cup)
  • 2 cups quick-cooking long-grain and wild rice blend
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Gruyère cheese, divided
  • 1 (8-oz) package cream cheese, cubed and softened
  • 1 1/2 cups half-and-half, divided
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 1/2 cups unsalted beef broth
  • 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp ground pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and lightly butter a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Stir the rice blend, beef broth, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, thyme, apple cider vinegar, and about 1 cup of the half-and-half together in the baking dish.
  3. Melt the butter in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the shallots and sauté until browned. Add the mushrooms with a little salt and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 8 minutes, until the liquid they release has cooked off.
  4. Turn off the heat and stir the cream cheese, remaining half-and-half, shredded chicken, and 1 cup of the Gruyère into the mushroom mixture until well combined.
  5. Add the mushroom mixture to the baking dish and stir until well combined.
  6. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 45 minutes.
  7. Uncover, top evenly with the remaining Gruyère, and bake 30 more minutes, until bubbly and golden on top.
  8. Let rest 10 minutes before serving, garnished with extra thyme leaves.
