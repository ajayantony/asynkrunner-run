Kerala Style Nadan Pork Fry (Ularthiyathu)

🍳 Food Kerala Moderate
15 min - 1 hrPrep
2 hrCook
8Servings
📖 Adapted from Maria's Menu

A classic Kerala-style dry pork roast in which the pork shines with just enough masala — finger-licking good. My favorite way to eat it, though, is paired with a crispy dosa — a match made in culinary heaven.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 500 g pork shoulder, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 5 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 4 green chillies, chopped
  • 3 tbsp pepper powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 4 tsp coriander powder
  • 2 tsp vinegar
  • 2/3 - 1 cup thengakothu (coconut bits)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Curry leaves
  • 24-30 shallots, sliced, or 2 large red onions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seeds
  • Coconut oil

Instructions

  1. Marinate the cleaned pork with the ginger garlic paste, green chillies, pepper powder, turmeric, coriander powder, vinegar, coconut bits, salt, and a few curry leaves. Let it sit for about 15 minutes to 1 hour.
  2. Transfer to a claypot (or a heavy-bottomed pot) along with 1/2 cup water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to the lowest flame.
  3. Cover and cook until the pork is done, about 45-60 minutes, adding small amounts of hot water between intervals if it starts sticking to the bottom.
  4. In a wide pan, heat coconut oil and let the mustard and fennel seeds crackle. Add the sliced shallots (or onions) and curry leaves and let them brown.
  5. Add the cooked pork along with any remaining liquid and mix well.
  6. Cook on low flame, stirring occasionally, until the pork is nicely roasted and turns a dark color. Add a little more coconut oil if it looks dry. This takes an hour or more.
  7. Taste and add more pepper powder if needed.
  8. Serve as a starter or alongside rice or parotta.
